✅ The Kanpur Citizen "DO's"

  • ✓ Rinse milk bags and food plastic containers before dry disposal.
  • ✓ Keep separate green and blue dustbins at your home entrance.
  • ✓ Hand over hazardous sanitary waste wrapped in paper marked with a red dot.
  • ✓ Carry your own cloth bag whenever visiting Kidwai Nagar or Naveen Market.

❌ The Kanpur Citizen "DON'Ts"

  • ✗ Never mix wet food scraps with dry paper or plastic recyclables.
  • ✗ Never throw waste into open roadside drains or the holy River Ganga.
  • ✗ Never burn dry leaves or plastic waste in the winter (Severe Air Quality Hazard).
  • ✗ Never dump construction malba on roadside public walking paths.
